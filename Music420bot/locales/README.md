# 🎵 Multi-Language Telegram Music & Tournament Bot

A feature-rich, high-performance, and lag-free Telegram Music Bot designed for seamless voice chat streaming, playlist management, and group tournaments.

## ✨ Features

- **Crystal Clear Audio**: High-fidelity Opus audio streaming with 24/7 uptime.
- **Smart Queue Management**: Smooth queue control supporting 30+ tracks seamlessly.
- **Multi-Assistant Architecture**: Automatically invites assistant accounts to handle voice calls.
- **Interactive Tournaments**: In-chat Solo and Team Battle gaming arenas with live leaderboards.
- **Multi-Language Support**: Fully localized with dynamic switching across 22 major languages.
